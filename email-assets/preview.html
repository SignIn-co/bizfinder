02 Health corner

Kennel cough around Auckland.

Kennel cough has been circulating more widely across Auckland over recent weeks. We want you to hear where we stand, directly from us.

The situation at Woofland remains below our threshold for declaring an outbreak. Even so, we have increased our cleaning, sanitisation and health monitoring, and we will tell you if that changes.

Enhanced cleaning
Extra washing and sanitisation of walls, doors, door handles, gates and frequently touched surfaces.
Bowls and feeding equipment
Food and water bowls are washed and sanitised more frequently.
Boarding pods
Additional cleaning and sanitisation of pods and sleeping areas.
Health monitoring
Closer attention to coughing, sneezing and unusual behaviour.
Early precautions
Dogs with possible respiratory symptoms are separated while we assess the situation and contact their families.
Communication
We contact families directly about any case involving their dog, and we follow up on every report we receive.
How you can help
Please keep your dog home if coughing or showing other respiratory symptoms. Keep canine cough vaccinations up to date, let us know about suspected exposure or diagnosis, and contact your vet if symptoms develop or worsen.

Kennel cough is not limited to kennels or daycare facilities. Dogs can be exposed anywhere they meet other dogs, from the park to the footpath.

We have written a full guide on our blog covering what kennel cough is, how it spreads, the signs to watch for and when to call your vet. Please take a few minutes to read it.

Thank you for helping us keep our Woofland family healthy and happy.

03 Holiday daycare

Yes, daycare is open.

Woofland Daycare continues operating through the festive season and New Year holiday period. Your dog can keep enjoying familiar playmates, activities and routines while you navigate the busy holidays.
